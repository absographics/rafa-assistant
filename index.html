🛡️ Hifazat (Security)

The assistant's security arrangements — all of these are always ON.

✓Scam detection — messages with fake links, OTP or payment tricks are flagged, never answered.ON
✓Fake-order block — a suspicious order is never confirmed without a call from the team.ON
✓Internal details stay sealed — the assistant never shares anything outside its book.ON
✓Message floods — if one number floods the chat, the assistant stops and passes it to the team.ON
✓Keys locked in the backend — API keys are never shown in the app.ON
✓Official connections only — WhatsApp and Instagram connect only through official routes.ON
✓Model protection — however cleverly someone asks, the assistant never reveals its internal instructions.ON
✓What isn't in the book can't leak — margins, suppliers and team matters are never put in the book.ON
✓Real login is on (Google) — the app doesn't open without a login.ON

In the live system this protection runs on the backend — in this demo, the detection part runs right here in the app.
